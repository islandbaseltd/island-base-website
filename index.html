Frequently asked questions.

Ask a question →
How do I request a booking?

Tap any “Book via WhatsApp” button and send your trip details. Island Base will review your request, confirm availability and provide a quotation. Your booking is confirmed only after you receive written confirmation.

What information must I provide?

Please send your full name, required service, date, pickup time and location, destination, one-way or return preference, passenger count, luggage details, additional stops, waiting requirements and any special requests.

Do you operate throughout Trinidad?

Yes. Private transportation is available throughout Trinidad. Final pricing depends on the route, distance, duration, traffic conditions and specific booking requirements.

Do you provide airport pickups and drop-offs?

Yes. Pre-booked transfers to and from Piarco International Airport are available, including one-way and return journeys. Please share your flight and luggage details when enquiring.

Can someone book medical transportation for a relative?

Yes. A family member may arrange a pre-booked, non-emergency journey for a loved one. Please provide accurate passenger, mobility, appointment and pickup details so suitability can be confirmed.

Do you offer one-way and round-trip transportation?

Yes. One-way, return, hourly, daily and multi-day bookings are available, depending on the service and schedule.

How many passengers and suitcases can you carry?

The Suzuki Fronx can normally accommodate up to four passengers with approximately two large suitcases and three smaller carry-on bags. For airport transfers, the recommended maximum is four passengers with medium luggage. Capacity depends on suitcase size and passenger count, so all passenger and luggage details must be confirmed before booking.

Are prices fixed?

Prices shown are starting or standard prices. Your final quotation can vary with distance, traffic, duration, waiting time, stops, time of day, public holidays and specific booking requirements. The final price is provided before confirmation.

Do you accept TTD and USD?

Yes. TTD cash, USD cash, local bank transfer and PayPal where agreed in advance are currently accepted. The quotation will clearly state the agreed currency and amount.

Can I add additional stops?

Yes. Share every planned stop when requesting your quote. An additional stop is another destination during the journey—for example, travelling from a cinema to a restaurant. Extra-stop charges may apply.

Do you offer hourly and full-day chauffeur services?

Yes. Hourly, 8-hour daily and multi-day packages are available. Send your itinerary and expected times so the best option can be quoted.

Can I request a customised itinerary?

Yes. Custom quotations are available for multiple stops, day trips, tours, long-distance journeys, special events and other tailored transport needs.

How early should I book?

At least 24 hours is preferred for standard trips. Please allow at least 48 hours for tours, events, full-day chauffeur service and long-distance trips. Same-day and short-notice requests may be accepted subject to availability and may attract an additional charge.

Are early-morning and late-night trips available?

Yes, with advance booking and subject to availability. Trips between 10:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m. may incur a 15% surcharge, disclosed in the quotation before confirmation.

Do you provide child seats?

No. Island Base does not currently provide child seats. Customers may bring an appropriate child seat; the parent or guardian is responsible for supplying, installing and checking it. Please disclose child-seat requirements before booking.

Can you accommodate mobility equipment?

Island Base operates a standard Suzuki Fronx, not a medical transport vehicle. Foldable walking aids or mobility equipment may be accepted depending on available luggage space and must be disclosed before booking. Passengers must be able to safely enter and exit a standard crossover vehicle.

Do you provide emergency medical transportation?

No. Island Base provides pre-booked, non-emergency private transportation and is not an ambulance or medical-care provider. For an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Before you book

Clear terms for a smooth journey.

Final prices, required deposits, surcharges and payment timing are disclosed in writing before a booking is confirmed.

Booking & deposits

A 30% deposit may be required for tours and beach trips, events, full-day or multi-day chauffeur bookings, long-distance or customised trips, and bookings valued at TT$400 or more.

Some smaller standard trips, including airport transfers and medical appointments, may be confirmed without a deposit at Island Base’s discretion. A required deposit is due when the quotation is accepted. The booking is confirmed only after the deposit is received and Island Base issues written confirmation.

Cancellations

  • More than 24 hours before pickup: the deposit may be refunded or transferred to another booking.
  • Between 12 and 24 hours: 50% of the deposit may be retained.
  • Less than 12 hours before pickup or a no-show: the deposit is non-refundable.
  • If Island Base must cancel and cannot provide an agreed replacement, all payments received will be refunded.

Please communicate changes and cancellations as early as possible.

Waiting time

Regular pickups: the first 15 minutes are complimentary. After that, TT$50 may be charged for each additional 30 minutes or part thereof, depending on the service and circumstances.

Airport pickups: when the correct flight number is supplied, flight delays will be monitored and up to 45 minutes after the actual arrival time is included. Additional waiting costs TT$50 per hour or part thereof.

Medical appointment waiting arrangements are quoted separately.

Late-night & holiday trips

Trips between 10:00 p.m. and 5:00 a.m. may incur a 15% surcharge. Public-holiday bookings may incur a 20% surcharge. Every applicable surcharge will be disclosed in the quotation before confirmation.

Early-morning and late-night transportation is available by advance reservation, subject to availability.

Payment

Accepted methods are TTD cash, USD cash, local bank transfer and PayPal where agreed in advance. The quotation will state the agreed currency and amount.

  • Required deposits are due when the quotation is accepted.
  • The remaining balance is due before, during or at the end of the service, as stated in the quotation.
  • Airport-arrival customers may pay the agreed balance at pickup.
  • Full-day, tour, event and multi-day bookings should normally be paid in full at least 24 hours before service.
  • Corporate payment terms may be approved separately in writing.

Children, accessibility & medical limits

Island Base does not currently provide child seats. Customers may bring an appropriate seat; the parent or guardian is responsible for supplying, installing and checking it. Requirements must be disclosed before booking.

Island Base currently operates a standard Suzuki Fronx and is not a medical transport vehicle. The service may accommodate passengers who can safely enter and exit a standard crossover. Foldable walking aids or mobility equipment may be accepted depending on luggage space and must be disclosed before booking. Island Base does not provide lifting, medical assistance or ambulance services.

Important

All bookings are subject to availability and accurate trip details. Changes to the route, duration, stops or timing may change the price. A booking is not confirmed until written confirmation is issued by Island Base.
