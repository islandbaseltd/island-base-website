Ask a question →
How much does a trip cost?

Every journey is quoted based on its complete service requirements. Submit your details to receive a personalized quotation.

How do quotations and bookings work?

Send your complete journey details. Island Base reviews them privately and sends a written quotation showing the total, inclusions, payment requirements and conditions. A booking is confirmed only after the quotation is accepted, any required reservation payment is received and Island Base issues written confirmation.

What information must I provide?

Please provide your name, service, date, pickup time and exact location, destination or return location, journey type, return-collection time where applicable, ordered stops, waiting estimate, passengers, luggage and service-specific details such as a flight number, beach choice or party venue. Do not send diagnoses, passwords, PINs or card information.

Are prices charged per person?

Standard private transportation is reserved for the customer or group, not sold as individual shared seats. Package terms and vehicle capacity will be stated in the quotation.

How many passengers can travel?

The current vehicle accommodates up to four passengers, subject to luggage and safe vehicle capacity.

Can I add stops after booking?

Additional stops are subject to availability and may require a revised quotation.

Do you offer airport flight monitoring?

Yes. Customers must provide an accurate flight number for arriving airport pickups.

Are food and drinks included in beach packages?

No. Drinks may be requested and quoted separately. Food, chairs, umbrellas, entrance fees, parking and activities are not included unless specifically stated.

Do you accept USD?

Customers requiring a USD quotation should mention this when submitting their request. Currency and payment terms will be stated in the written quotation.

Do you operate throughout Trinidad?

Yes. Pre-booked private transportation is available throughout Trinidad, subject to availability, safety and vehicle suitability.

Do you provide child seats?

No. Island Base does not currently provide child seats. Customers may bring an appropriate child seat; the parent or guardian is responsible for supplying, installing and checking it. Please disclose the requirement before booking.

Can you accommodate mobility equipment?

Foldable walking aids or mobility equipment may be accepted depending on available luggage space and must be disclosed before booking. Passengers must be able to safely enter and exit a standard crossover vehicle.

Do you provide emergency medical transportation?

No. Island Base provides pre-booked non-emergency transportation and is not an ambulance or medical-supervision service. For an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service.

Before you book

Clear terms for a smooth journey.

Your written quotation states the total, inclusions, reservation payment, balance deadline and service-specific conditions before confirmation.

Reservation payments

Some services require a reservation payment before the booking can be confirmed. Beach and party packages require a 25% reservation payment, which is credited toward the total quotation.

Any required reservation payment and deadline will be stated in writing. A booking is not confirmed until the required payment is received and Island Base issues written confirmation.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Reservation payments are normally non-refundable because the agreed time and vehicle are held for the customer. Beach-package customers may request one reschedule with at least 48 hours’ notice, subject to availability. Less notice may result in forfeiture of the reservation payment.

Where severe weather prevents a beach trip from proceeding safely, one reschedule may be offered. If Island Base cancels, the customer may receive a refund or transfer the payment to another available date. Party cancellation terms are stated in each written quotation.

Waiting & airport collection

Reasonable waiting may be included where stated in your quotation. Additional waiting beyond the agreed allowance may result in an additional charge. Please provide realistic waiting or return-collection information.

For airport pickups, Island Base monitors the supplied flight number and includes a reasonable collection allowance for normal arrival procedures. Extended delays, missing flight information or delays unrelated to the flight may affect the final charge.

Stops, timing & special dates

All stops must be disclosed before quotation and listed in order. Later additions are subject to availability and may require a revised quotation.

Late-night service depends on chauffeur availability, customer verification, pickup location and safety. Public holidays, Carnival, Christmas and New Year are subject to limited availability and custom quotation. Short-notice adjustments are included in the quotation before acceptance.

Payment & currency

Your quotation will state any reservation payment, remaining balance and deadlines. Package balances must be paid by the deadline in the written confirmation.

Quotations are issued in the currency stated on the written quotation. Customers requiring a USD quotation should request it when submitting their details. Payment method, currency and applicable terms are confirmed before booking.

Children, accessibility & medical limits

Island Base does not currently provide child seats. Customers may bring an appropriate seat; the parent or guardian is responsible for supplying, installing and checking it. Requirements must be disclosed before booking.

Island Base currently operates a standard Suzuki Fronx and is not a medical transport vehicle. The service may accommodate passengers who can safely enter and exit a standard crossover. Foldable walking aids or mobility equipment may be accepted depending on luggage space and must be disclosed before booking. Island Base does not provide lifting, medical assistance or ambulance services.

Important

All bookings are subject to availability, safety, vehicle suitability and accurate details. Changes to the route, timing, stops or requirements may need a revised quotation. A booking is not confirmed until Island Base issues written confirmation.
